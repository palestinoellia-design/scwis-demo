Three Core Portals

Student, Counselor, and Administrator systems connected through one secure platform.

Each portal supports interactive workflows including consent management, AI analytics, human review, interventions, follow-up, and audit tracking.

S

Student Portal

Students can complete wellness check-ins, manage consent settings, and access personal wellness resources.

  • Self-assessment check-ins
  • Consent management
  • Personal wellness reports
  • Support resources & emergency contact
C

Counselor Dashboard

Counselors review AI-generated alerts, verify risk explanations, and manage intervention workflows.

  • Risk monitoring dashboard
  • Human review approval
  • Tiered interventions
  • Case tracking & follow-up
A

Administrator Panel

Administrators manage system configuration, data imports, and campus-wide analytics.

  • CSV data import
  • Campus analytics
  • Risk threshold settings
  • Trust & audit dashboard
Workflow

Data Collection → AI Detection → Counselor Review → Tiered Intervention → Follow-up

The system never performs automatic intervention. All actions require counselor review and approval.
